🚌 Transportation

We know transportation is top of mind, so here is exactly how it works.

Transportation for Students With an IEP

Transportation is provided for students whose IEP includes it. If your student qualifies, you do not need to do anything to get started. Our team will reach out to you directly.

Mr. Brazile, our Assistant Principal of Special Education, and his team will personally call every family that qualifies. Please keep an eye out for their call.

Bus tickets are a separate program, described below.

Bus Tickets

We will have a limited number of bus tickets at the start of the year, with more arriving in our next shipment.

Who is eligible: Students who live in Newark and at least 2.5 miles from school.

How they work: The city currently provides tickets once a month for each eligible student, so students should use them responsibly to last the whole month.

Have a transportation question? We've got you.

IEP transportation: Mr. Brazile, Assistant Principal of Special Education, at DBrazile@kippnj.org.

Bus tickets: Samantha Scott, our School Operations Manager, at SScott4@kippnj.org.
👕 Everyday Dress Code
Students are expected to be in full dress code every single day.
✓  Tops: Polo with school logo, the school gym t-shirt from FlynnO'Hara (any day), or any top with the official school logo in our school colors of navy blue, light blue, white, or grey
✓  Bottoms: Grey slacks
✓  Headgear: Religious and cultural headwear are welcome
✓  Shoes: Rubber-bottom, closed-toe shoes
✓  Outerwear: Must be school branded
✗  Not permitted: Jeans, shorts, sweatpants, skirts, skorts, tights, leggings, hoods, hats, flip flops, sandals, and any open-toed or open-heeled shoes

🎯 Aiming High Together

We set the bar high because we believe in every single one of our students. Here is what we are chasing together this year, and we will be right beside your student every step of the way.

✏️ Homework every day

Expect your student to bring home work each day. A little every night keeps the learning going and builds the habits of a college-bound scholar.

📅 Show up and shine: 94%

94% is our team attendance goal, and it is a number we hit together. Every day your Panther is here is a day they get to grow, laugh, and level up. We honestly cannot wait to see them. Here, present, and ready is the whole game.

⭐ Aim for a 3.0 GPA

Our goal is for every student to earn a GPA of 3.0 or higher, which is an 83% or better. It is absolutely within reach, and our teachers and team are here to help your student get there.
